## What this does not show

- One device, one board, one temperature. Not multi-corner, not multi-board.
- An FPGA, not an ASIC. No figure here predicts ASIC power, area or frequency.
- The workload is a 2-2-1 network learning XOR. It is a training primitive, not a production accelerator, and says nothing about tokens per second.
- Throughput "as run" includes the UART and the host. It is not the core's peak rate.
- The open flow cannot express a multicycle constraint. If the trainer bitstream was built with `--timing-allow-fail`, its correct behaviour is a property of this placement and seed (see `gHashTag/t27/docs/SILICON_TRAINING_METHODOLOGY.md`).
- CFGMCLK is an internal oscillator. Its frequency differs from die to die (68.8–70.77 MHz across three dice in the repo record) and drifts with temperature.
