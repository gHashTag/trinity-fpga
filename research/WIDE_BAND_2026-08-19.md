# Wide-band accumulation: where TNF's range ends and tekum's taper pays — 2026-08-19

This closes the one unmeasured cell of `research/TEKUM_VS_TNF_LINE_2026-08-19.md`
§6: every accuracy number in the line was taken on a single ±3-decade band,
inside every contender's range. TNF(4,8) spans a finite window and then
hard-clips; tekum's tapered exponent keeps going. No benchmark had exercised
the band past TNF's span. `conformance/wide_band_bench.py` does.

## Method

Identical harness to `conformance/tekum_true_bench.py` (its `acc`/`acc_tekum`
are imported and reused for the anchor): 64-term accumulation, seed 11, the
running sum re-quantised through the format after every add, identical samples
for all formats per trial, 60 trials per band (≥ 40 required). Bands:
log-uniform magnitudes over ±3, ±6, ±9, ±11, ±13, ±16, ±20, ±30 decades.
Formats: TNF(4,8), takum16 (the linear structural model of `takum_ref.py`,
post-#606), tekum10 (true base-3, 10 trits = 15.85 stored bits).

**Anchor verified before widening**: the ±3 band re-run through the parent
bench's own functions reproduces its recorded means exactly —
tekum10 8.561e-03, takum16 5.697e-03, TNF(4,8) 5.323e-03.

**One deliberate deviation, stated openly**: the parent bench converts inputs
with `Fraction(x).limit_denominator(10**12)`, which maps every magnitude below
~1e-12 to exactly zero. At ±3 decades that is harmless float hygiene; past
±12 decades it erases the low half of the band — the instrument would lie
inside the failure domain it is supposed to measure. The wide bands therefore
convert with bare `Fraction(x)` (a binary float IS an exact rational). Both
pipelines were run at ±3 and agree to all printed digits, which is the license
to widen.

**TNF's encode on overflow, explicitly**: any value whose exponent offset
reaches `offset_max = 3^4 − 1 = 80` returns `(sign << 15) | (80 << 8)` — the
reserved special row, zero mantissa — and `decode()` maps that word to ±inf
(a float, not a Fraction). Detected with `tnf_ref.is_special()` on the freshly
encoded word at every step. Underflow: no subnormals; |v| ≤ 2^−40 encodes the
bare sign bit (the zero code), counted as a **flush** when the pre-quantisation
value was nonzero.

**Accounting** (repo discipline: failures are never folded into a mean).
Per band and format: (a) mean rel err over trials the format finished finite
and in range; (b) failed trials, split special (overflow row/NaR/inf) +
flush (running sum quantised to zero while the true sum is nonzero) +
sat (pre-quantisation magnitude above the largest finite — catches silent
clamping, since takum16's encode saturates to its top code and tekum's
bisection can only return finite codes); (c) element clip rate: fraction of
INPUT samples strictly outside the format's representable magnitude range.

Ranges from code-space scans, not formulas (takum16: all 65,536 codes;
tekum10: extreme finite codes, monotonicity proven in its selftest):

| format | min_pos | max_finite | log10 span |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| TNF(4,8) | 1.819e-12 | 1.097e+12 | [−11.74, +12.04] |
| takum16 | 1.835e-77 | 5.609e+76 | [−76.74, +76.75] |
| tekum10 | 4.322e-88 | 2.285e+87 | [−87.36, +87.36] |

Note: tekum10's measured code-space span is ±87.4 decades — narrower than the
±174-decade figure §6 extrapolated from the RTL range-check bound (e ∈ ±365 in
base 3 is what the *check* admits, not what the anchored code space reaches).

difficulty is not constant.

## The crossover

**TNF(4,8) is fully usable through ±11 decades** (0 failures in 60 trials,
0.36% of inputs already clipped but absorbed by rounding) **and stops being
usable at ±13** (50/60 trials failed: 46 overflowed to the special row, 4
flushed a nonzero running sum to zero). From ±16 on, every trial fails. The
crossover sits between ±11 and ±13 decades — exactly where the code-space scan
says its span ends ([1.8e-12, 1.1e12], half-span ~±11.9 decades). The expected
shape from §6 is confirmed on both sides: inside ±11 the three formats tie
(within 1.6–5×, ordering shuffling between bands), and past TNF's span
tekum10 and takum16 degrade smoothly — zero failures, zero saturations, zero
input clips all the way to ±30 decades, errors staying in the 1e-2 class.

takum16's own range (ends at ±76.7 decades) is never reached: no band's inputs
clip (0.00% everywhere; a 64-term sum of ±30-decade samples is bounded by
6.4e31, forty-five decades short of its 5.6e76 ceiling). tekum10 likewise. To
make either tapered format fail this harness the band would have to widen past
±76 decades.

One honest surprise, reported because it is against no one's thesis: at ±6
tekum10's full-population mean is 5–6× better than both binary formats —
its taper carries 7 fraction trits (~11.1 bits) in the central regimes against
TNF's fixed 8 bits, and that shows on the cancellation-heavy trials that
dominate the mean. The medians (2.1/4.3/3.7e-03) stay a three-way tie.

## What this buys tekum for the 38× hardware price

This is the workload §6 predicted and the first one measured where tekum
(and takum) beat TNF outright: past ±12 decades TNF(4,8) is not less accurate,
it is *absent* — 83–100% of trials end in the special row or a flushed sum —
while tekum10 finishes every trial out to ±30 decades at 1e-2-class error with
0.15 fewer stored bits. That graceful degradation over a ~7× wider magnitude
span (±87 vs ±12 decades) is exactly what tekum's tapered base-3 exponent
purchases with its 38× LUT entry price (`fpga/tekum/TEKUM_HW.md`); whether any
real workload occupies more than ±11 decades of dynamic range in a 16-bit
format is the question this benchmark cannot answer. Note takum16 — binary,
and by the line's own hardware datapoint ~3× cheaper than tekum-class RTL —
degrades just as gracefully here, so wide-band robustness alone does not
argue for base 3.

## Caveats

* One seed (11), one trial shape (64-term accumulation), 60 trials/band.
  Means are tail-dominated; medians are given above for context.
* takum16 is the **linear structural model** of `takum_ref.py`, not the
  logarithmic takum as published; tekum10 is true base-3 per
  `tekum_true_ref.py` (its two ambiguous special codes are never touched
  here — encode only returns finite codes).
* The ±13 TNF mean is a survivor subset (10/60 trials) and must not be
  quoted as "TNF's error at ±13".
* Input clip (c) counts samples strictly outside [min_pos, max_finite];
  values just below min_pos that round up to it are not counted, values in
  the one-half-ULP sliver above max_finite that round down are not counted.
* Branch note: `tnf_ref.TRUE_LADDER` does not exist on this branch, so the
  32-bit half of `tekum_true_bench.py` crashes after printing its (correct,
  reproduced) 16-bit table. Not touched here; the 16-bit anchor is unaffected.
