# The competitor found, and what it shows about our own claim

A literature sweep for multiplier-free ternary hardware surfaced the nearest
prior art to this paper's central claim, which the paper had not cited.

## FQP (DAC 2024)

Yang, Wang, Qin, Wang, Wei, Hu and Yin, *FQP: A Fibonacci Quantization
Processor with Multiplication-Free Computing and Topological-Order Routing*,
61st ACM/IEEE Design Automation Conference. Quantizes weights to Fibonacci
integers and turns their products into additions, using two arithmetic units --
a dualistic-transformation adder for large operands, a bit-exclusive adder for
small ones -- plus a topological-order routing scheme mapping data onto them.

A top venue, a real processor, and the same aim. It should have been cited, and
now is.

## What the comparison gave us

The first reading of a strong competitor is that it narrows our claim. The
second reading, which is the correct one here, is that it explains it.

FQP needs a transformation stage and a scheduler. Ours needs neither. That is
not a difference in engineering effort, and it does not follow from anything we
did well. It follows from a property of the two number sets:

- Fibonacci integers are not closed under multiplication. $F_4 F_4 = 9$, and $9$
  is not a Fibonacci number. Every product leaves the representable set and must
  be transformed back into it -- hence the *dualistic transformation*, hence the
  routing that feeds it.
- $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$ **is** closed under multiplication by $\varphi$:
  $\varphi(a+b\varphi) = b + (a+b)\varphi$. The product never leaves. One
  integer addition, no stage to transform, nothing to schedule.

The distinction is between quantizing to Fibonacci *numbers* and working in the
ring those numbers are the *coordinates* of. It is one sentence, and it is the
whole difference in the datapath.

## Corollary (closure removes the transformation stage)

Let $A$ be a weight alphabet and $s$ the operation applying a weight. If the
additive group generated by $A$ is closed under $s$, the datapath needs only
that group's addition. If not, every product must be re-expressed in the
representable set, requiring a transformation stage and a schedule routing
operands to it.

Now in the paper as Corollary~\ref{cor:closure}, proved from the existing
exactness theorem and its negation. The closure half is machine-checked in Coq
with no admitted lemmas.

## Also found

Fiandaca and Gomony, *Fibbinary-Based Compression and Quantization for Efficient
Neural Radio Receivers*, arXiv:2511.01921 (Nov 2025). Reports 45% and 44%
savings in multiplier power and area. The multiplier is made cheaper, not
removed -- the useful contrast for a paper whose subtitle claims no multiplier
anywhere. Our bibliography already carried this correctly; a search engine
attributed it to a different author, and checking our own file rather than
trusting the summary is what showed the citation was sound.

## The methodological note

Forty-three iterations of self-audit found eighteen defects in our own
measurements and none in the literature position, because the literature
position had never been searched. An instrument pointed only inward reports
only inward faults. The competitor sweep took two queries.
