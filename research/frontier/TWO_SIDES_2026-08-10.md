# The two sides of a node want different rungs

Everything measured so far is weights. Activations are the other operand, and
the synthetic sweep already said what to look for: the optimal ratio rises
monotonically with tail weight, so if activations are heavier-tailed they want a
coarser ladder.

Measured on SmolLM2-135M, inputs of every linear projection captured over two
windows of 2048 tokens, normalised per row exactly as weights are normalised per
output channel:

| | excess kurtosis | fraction below 1/64 | r*(3b) | r*(4b) | r*(5b) | r*(6b) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| weights | 1.44 | 4.96% | 2.0535 | 1.4160 | 1.2022 | 1.1071 |
| **activations** | **33.55** | **30.86%** | 2.6000 | **1.6853** | 1.3051 | 1.1507 |

Activations are **23x** heavier-tailed and hold **six times** as much mass below
1/64: a sharp peak at zero with a long tail, against the weights' comparatively
compact distribution. The optimal ratio rises accordingly at every budget.

Reading it against the hierarchy:

| bits | weights want | activations want |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | shift | shift |
| 4 | supergolden (deg 3) | **phi (deg 2)** |
| 5 | degree 4 | plastic (deg 3) |
| 6 | degree 4 | degree 4 |

**T35 (the two-sided ladder).** The operands of a quantised layer do not share
an optimal ladder. Activations, being heavier-tailed, want a coarser rung than
weights at the same budget -- one degree lower over the range measured, which is
one register cheaper.

## What this does and does not say

**Does.** If both operands of a layer are put on multiply-free ladders, they
should not be put on the same one, and the activation side is the cheaper of the
two. At four bits it wants exactly the alphabet this paper uses.

**Does not.** Our own datapath does not quantise activations to a ladder -- the
sample arrives ADC-native and is used as it lands, which is the whole point of
the three-site argument. This measures what a ladder would cost on that side if
one were imposed, and finds that the answer is our rung, not that our design
needs one.

The result is a fact about quantised layers generally, and it happens to point
at `phi` on the side we do not quantise. It is worth stating for the same reason
the plastic-number result was worth stating when it went against us: the
hierarchy makes predictions on both operands, and reporting only the operand
where we win would be choosing the measurement after seeing it.
