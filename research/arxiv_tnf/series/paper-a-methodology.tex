\begin{abstract}
Comparing number formats in hardware is harder than measuring them, and the two
failures are independent: nearly every difficulty encountered in a three-year
FPGA campaign left the measurement intact and broke the comparison built around
it. This paper states the regularities, because they generalise past the formats
that occasioned them and because each is a condition under which a ratio between
two designs means anything at all.

Two are measured here for the first time. \textbf{Synthesis deletes the checks
whose area is being measured.} A census over seven on-die wrapper sources finds
fourteen of twenty-eight assertion clauses folded to constants --- a folded
clause reads PASS in every build, including the failing ones --- and repairing
the folding raises the routed design from $452$ to $696$ LUT, so thirty-five per
cent of the design was absent from the figures published as its area. Two
previously published verdicts change meaning in consequence: a design reported
as passing four of four clauses passed one of one actually evaluated, and a
design reported as failing two of four fails every clause it evaluates. Marking
the probe register \texttt{(* keep *)} does not fix this; \texttt{keep}
preserves the cell and the optimiser propagates the constant into the comparison
regardless.

\textbf{Achieved frequency is a proxy for size.} Across eleven designs spanning
a $39.6\times$ range of area, five placer seeds each,
$F_{\max}=4397\cdot\mathrm{LUT}^{-0.648}$ at $R^2=0.920$; the exponent is
unchanged when the fit is taken over all fifty-five individual seed points
rather than the medians. A metric of the form ``throughput per unit area''
therefore divides a quantity by a second that is already a function of it: on
our own combined harness, ranking by throughput per area reproduced ranking by
inverse area at a Spearman correlation of $0.961$. With seed-to-seed spread
reaching $20.5\%$ on a single design, such a ranking resolves only rows
separated by more than that.

Four further regularities concern what is being compared, the instrument, the
harness, and the sources. Applied to two external papers read in full, the
procedure produced no findings against them and caught our own reading of them
on both occasions; we therefore offer it as an instrument for checking one's own
work and not for auditing anyone else's. Three of the checks are mechanised as
continuous-integration gates. Sixteen claims of our own were withdrawn under it,
and the withdrawals are marked in place.
\end{abstract}
