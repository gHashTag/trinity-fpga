A ternary node has three sites that could carry a number format; one needs to. The weight is a code: multiplying by it is a sign-select. The sample arrives ADC-native. The accumulator alone has a range to spend, and Ternary Network Floats (TNF) spend it -- a sign, a balanced-ternary exponent of Et trits, an M-bit mantissa, with 1+Et+M=N. GFTernary, a two-bit \-phi,0,+phi\ alphabet, carries the weight, and phi is forced: it is the unique r>1 with r^2=r+1, so the lattice Z[phi] is closed under both the weight operation and accumulation, and the gain of k layers is exactly Fkphi+F_k-1. Closure removes a multiplier rather than cheapening it: a set lacking it must return every accumulation to the set, a stage costing 9.6x the whole closed accumulation at 16 bits and 24.8x at 32. On an XC7A200T in a fully open flow the pair costs zero DSP: a matched filter on real air at 78 MHz, every transformer block, a systolic GEMM at 33 GOPS, a trained ternary transformer at 97%, and on-silicon backpropagation training XOR to 4/4. Among formats carrying their own range, ours are first and second on throughput per area, 0.177 and 0.167 MHz/LUT against binary32's 0.163. The ladder's reach is smaller than a position count suggests. Apparent slack of 0.3691E over a uniform binary budget vanishes once positions are counted as bits: a realisable member obeys 3^Et2^M<= 2^N-1, and _2 3 _3 x = _2 x collapses the condition onto that budget exactly. At equal storage the family ties every uniform binary format that spends all its bits, dominates the 6 of 17 sampled that waste budget, and is escaped by tapers where they concentrate precision. The accumulator obeys a law we derive and measure: error grows as sqrtpK 2^-(M+1) in fan-in, fitted at +0.476 against a predicted +0.5.
