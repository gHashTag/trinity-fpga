## Verdict ladder

The ladder is fixed in the spec. For each comparator C, r = 1000 · ppl(T27_STAR) / ppl(C):
- r ≤ 990: T27 better;
- r ≤ 1010: tie;
- otherwise: C better.

| comparator | test r (‰) | test verdict | dev r (‰) | trits per 32, T27 vs C |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| **NVFP4 (primary)** | **932.4** | **T27 better** | 926.0 | 101 vs 106 |
| MXPLUS | 906.1 | T27 better | 907.9 | 101 vs 106 |

**Primary verdict: T27 better than NVFP4, on a trit substrate.** In the spec's words, "T27_STAR

## What it says

- A 3-trit cell with 27 levels and one power-of-two scale per 32 weights gives lower perplexity
  than NVFP4 on SmolLM2-135M. NVFP4 uses E2M1 per 16 with an E4M3 block scale and an FP32 tensor
  scale. On a trit substrate, T27 also uses 5 fewer trits per 32 weights.
- The mechanism is the one the spec gave as the reason for its guess. E2M1's 16 codes fill a
  3-trit cell's 27 only in part, and T27 uses all 27. That is about 0.75 bit more per element,
  and it outweighs NVFP4's finer, 16-wide E4M3 scale.
- T27 closes 40 % of NVFP4's gap to the unquantised model. The excess perplexity is 1.80 for T27
  against 2.98 for NVFP4.

## What it does not say

- **Nothing about binary memory.**
  - In bits, T27 costs 161 per 32 weights, packed densely, against NVFP4's 144. That is 12 % more.
  - E2M2 costs 168 bits and beats T27.
  - On a bit substrate, this is not an equal-storage comparison.
- **Nothing about the cost of a trit cell against a bit cell.** Nobody has measured it, and ReTern
  argues it is higher.
- **Nothing about other models, other corpora, activations or KV cache.** This is one 135M model,
  wikitext-2, and weights only.
- **No uncertainty interval.**
  - The ladder is a point comparison on 40 windows of 2048 tokens.
  - A per-window paired bootstrap was not pre-registered, so none is claimed.
  - The margins (932‰ and 906‰ against a 990‰ bar) are large, but that is judgement, not a test.
- **MX+ and NVFP4 are re-implementations**, from the paper and from NVIDIA's published recipe,
  not from the authors' code. The hand cases and grid-property unit checks passed.
  - MX+ uses the OCP scale rule, floor(log2 amax) − 2.
  - The MXFP4 row uses block_tnf's rule B, a ceil E8M0 scale.
  - Both rules are stated in the spec.
- **One of three T27 candidates was chosen on dev.** Test was touched once, after the choice.

## Anomalies recorded (none changes a number)

- **Window count in the log.** The line `windows test 148, dev 130` counts the windows available in
  each split. Perplexity uses `NWIN = 40`, a shared constant checked equal to the first attempt's.
  The log line is misleading but harmless. The runner was not edited after the run.
- **Slow test arms.** On test, NVFP4 took 48.6 s and MXPLUS 58.3 s, where other arms took about 31 s.
  The host's 15-minute load average was about 20 at 11:53Z. Perplexity here is deterministic: the
  rulers reproduced to 0.0000. So timing does not move a number.
